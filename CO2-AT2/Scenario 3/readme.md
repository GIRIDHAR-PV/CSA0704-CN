# Survey Your Own Wi-Fi World

## Wi-Fi Survey Analysis & Observations

### 1. Understanding Signal Strength & Network Congestion
* **Signal Strength:** Signals operating above **70% (-30 to -60 dBm)** deliver fast speeds and low latency. When signal strength drops below **40% (-75 dBm or lower)**, data packets get lost, forcing retransmissions that lead to buffering and lag.
* **Network Congestion:** The **2.4 GHz band** is heavily congested because it only offers 3 non-overlapping channels (1, 6, and 11). Nearby networks broadcasting on these same channels collide and interfere with one another. While the **5 GHz band** provides more channels and cleaner throughput, its higher frequencies struggle to penetrate thick walls and long distances.

---

### 2. Why Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) Solves Crowded Network Bottlenecks
In dense environments like hostels, auditoriums, or classrooms, legacy Wi-Fi (Wi-Fi 4/5) struggles because it communicates with devices sequentially—acting like a single delivery truck making one stop at a time while every other device waits in line.

Upgrading to **Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax)** fixes this bottleneck using two core technologies:
* **OFDMA (Orthogonal Frequency Division Multiple Access):** Subdivides channels into smaller sub-channels, allowing a router to transmit data to **multiple devices simultaneously** in a single transmission burst.
* **MU-MIMO (Multi-User, Multiple-Input, Multiple-Output):** Expands multi-user spatial streams, allowing simultaneous upload/download streams across up to 8 devices at once.

> **Takeaway:** In high-density settings, Wi-Fi 6 eliminates data queue wait times, significantly reduces latency, and maintains high internet speeds even when hundreds of devices are connected concurrently.
